About Me

I'm Aditya, a Full-Stack AI Engineer based in Seattle, WA. I build LLM-powered systems — agents, retrieval pipelines, data platforms — with a focus on making them reliable enough to actually ship.

My background spans research and industry: NLP work at USC, applied ML at RTI International across healthcare and policy use cases, AI architecture at Raven for a catalog of over a million items, and currently at EasyAUM, building financial intelligence tools that translate complex portfolio data into natural language. I care about the full picture — modeling, infrastructure, and the interface layer between them.

Outside of work I'm usually playing RPGs, doing digital art, or listening to whatever the algorithm throws at me. Good software and good design aren't that different — both reward attention to detail.

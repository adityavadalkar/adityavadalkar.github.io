Opioid Mortality Prediction Model

"predicting to prevent"

Implemented regression models (CatBoost, XGBoost, Random Forest) to explain 99.5% variance in opioid mortality in the US.

Technologies

PythonRShinyScikit-learn

About This Project

Developed high-accuracy machine learning models to predict and explain opioid mortality rates across US counties.

The ensemble approach using CatBoost, XGBoost, and Random Forest achieves 99.5% variance explanation, helping identify key factors contributing to opioid deaths.

This research supports public health interventions by highlighting which communities are most at risk and what factors drive mortality rates.
